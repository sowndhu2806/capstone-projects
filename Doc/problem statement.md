Problem Statement
1. Title
Smart Warranty Portal for Laptop Services
2. Domain
Laptop Warranty & Service Management
3. Who is the User?
Admin
Manage customers and laptops
Manage warranty and service requests
Customer
Register laptop
Check warranty
Raise service request
Track service status
4. What Problem Are We Solving?
Local laptop shops often manage warranty and service records manually. This makes it difficult to track warranty details and service requests. Customers may also have difficulty knowing their warranty status and repair progress.
5. Proposed Solution
A web-based portal that helps local laptop shops manage laptop details, warranty information, bills, and service requests digitally.

Users
Products
Warranties
ServiceRequests

Easy service request submission
Service status tracking
